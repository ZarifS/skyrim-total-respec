[center][size=6][b]Total Respec[/b][/size]
[i]A clean, one-potion character respec for Skyrim SE/AE — by OriginalZee[/i][/center]


[size=5][b]What this mod is[/b][/size]

[b]Total Respec[/b] gives you a single potion — the [b]Draught of Renewal[/b] — that fully respecs your character using Skyrim's [i]own[/i] systems, not a bolted-on custom menu.

Drink it, confirm, and the mod:
[list=1]
[*][b]Refunds every perk you've ever spent[/b], across all 18 skill trees.
[*][b]Resets your base Health, Magicka, Stamina, and Carry Weight[/b] to configurable baselines.
[*][b]Re-runs Skyrim's native level-up flow[/b] so you re-pick your attributes through the vanilla Skills > "Level Up!" screen.
[/list]
Your skill [i]levels[/i] are never touched — Smithing 80 stays Smithing 80. Only perks and attribute allocations are reset, exactly the things a respec should change.


[size=5][b]How it works[/b][/size]

[b]Perk refund.[/b] Rather than a hardcoded list of vanilla perks, Total Respec uses SKSE's ActorValueInfo.GetPerkTree to walk every skill tree and find the perks you actually have — so perks added by overhauls (Ordinator, Vokrii, Adamant, etc.) are refunded too, not just vanilla ones. It also [i]preserves bonus perk points[/i]: if you gained extra points from something like the Oghma Infinium, the refund math keeps them and only returns legitimately-spent points.

[b]Attribute reset, the engine-native way.[/b] Instead of scripting attribute math (which is fragile and fights attribute-rebasing mods), Total Respec drops your level to 1, sets your base H/M/S/CW to the configured baselines, then restores your original XP. Skyrim responds by queueing up all your level-ups again — and you re-allocate one attribute point per level through the standard level-up screen, the same robust path the game uses normally. It also carefully accounts for the +5 Carry Weight that each Stamina pick grants, so nothing drifts across the reset.

[b]Configurable baselines.[/b] The reset targets are read from Data/SKSE/Plugins/TotalRespec.json:
[code]
{
  "baselineHealth": 100,
  "baselineMagicka": 100,
  "baselineStamina": 100,
  "baselineCarryWeight": 300
}
[/code]
Defaults are vanilla race-base values. If you run an attribute-rebasing mod (e.g. SkyValor) or a custom race, set these to match and your modded base values are preserved through the respec.


[size=5][b]Where you get it[/b][/size]

The Draught of Renewal is sold [b]only by the Khajiit caravans[/b] — Ri'saad's traders that travel between the holds (Ahkari, Ma'randru-jo, and Ri'saad's groups). It is intentionally exclusive to them; no other merchant stocks it. Caravan inventory refreshes every couple of in-game days, so check back if it isn't currently listed.

[i](Technical note: there is no caravan-exclusive leveled list in vanilla — the caravans share general-vendor lists with every other merchant — so distribution is done by injecting the potion directly into the three caravan merchant chests via SkyPatcher. That's what keeps it caravan-only.)[/i]


[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Skyrim SE/AE[/b] (runtime 1.5.97 or 1.6.x)
[*][b]SKSE64[/b]
[*][b]JContainers SE[/b] — reads the JSON baseline config
[*][b]SkyPatcher[/b] — places the potion on the Khajiit caravans
[/list]
No SkyUI/MCM required.


[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Install the requirements above.
[*]Install Total Respec with your mod manager and enable TotalRespec.esp.
[*](Optional) edit Data/SKSE/Plugins/TotalRespec.json to match your attribute setup.
[*]Buy the [b]Draught of Renewal[/b] from a Khajiit caravan.
[*]Drink it, confirm, then open the Skills menu and click "Level Up!" for each queued level to re-allocate H/M/S.
[/list]
[b]Survival Mode:[/b] the level-up menu only opens after sleeping — sleep in a bed after drinking to access your queued level-ups.


[size=5][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Perk overhauls[/b] (Ordinator / Vokrii / Adamant / etc.) — supported automatically; any perk attached to a vanilla skill tree is refunded.
[*][b]Attribute-rebasing mods[/b] (SkyValor, custom races) — set the JSON baselines to match and your modded bases are preserved.
[*][b]Skill levels, gear, enchantments, standing stones[/b] — untouched; the respec only resets perks and base H/M/S/CW.
[/list]
